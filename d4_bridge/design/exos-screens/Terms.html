Exos connects fans with independent events and gives organizers the tools to sell and scan tickets. These terms cover both sides. Plain language; binding all the same.

Your ticket

  • A ticket is a revocable license to attend, not a transfer of property in the event.
  • Barcodes rotate every 30 seconds and are verified server-side. Screenshots don't scan — bring the live pass.
  • You may transfer a ticket for free to another person; the barcode reissues to them and yours voids.
  • Reselling above face value is prohibited except through official face-value exchange where enabled.

Refunds & changes

If an organizer cancels an event, you're refunded to your original payment method. If an event is rescheduled, your ticket stays valid for the new date; you'll be notified and may request a refund within the window the organizer sets. Fees are refunded on cancellation, not on voluntary transfers.

Organizer obligations

  • You must have the right to sell tickets to the events you list.
  • You're responsible for the event happening as described and for honoring valid tickets at the door.
  • Payouts settle through Stripe on the schedule shown in your dashboard, minus platform and processing fees.

Conduct

No fraud, no barcode duplication, no scraping, no reselling access to the platform. We void tickets and suspend accounts tied to fraudulent scans or chargebacks.

Liability

Exos is the ticketing technology, not the event producer. To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the amount you paid for the affected ticket. Nothing here waives rights you can't legally waive.
