PRIVACY
POLICY

Last updated 04 Jul 2026 · effective immediately

Exos is a ticketing platform operated by S4K Entertainment. This policy explains what we collect when you buy, hold, or transfer a ticket, and what organizers can see. We keep it short because you should be able to read it.

What we collect

  • Account basics — name, email, and the auth token from your sign-in provider.
  • Ticket activity — events you buy, save, transfer, or get scanned into.
  • Device + delivery data — so rotating QR codes verify and passes reach the right phone.
  • Payment metadata from Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

What organizers see

When you buy a ticket, the event's organizer sees your name, email, ticket tier, and check-in status — the same as a name at a will-call desk. They do not see your other events, your saved list, or your payment details.

What we never do

  • Sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.
  • Share your buying history across unrelated organizers.
  • Email you marketing you didn't opt into from that specific organizer.

Your controls

Export or delete your account any time from Profile → Settings. Deleting removes your personal data within 30 days; anonymized transaction records are retained where law requires. Cookie and analytics consent is managed from the banner and can be changed whenever you like.

Contact

Questions go to privacy@exos.live. If you're in the EU or California, you have additional rights (access, portability, erasure) and we honor all of them.
