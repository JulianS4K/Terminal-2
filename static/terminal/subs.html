Every open order that has already failed, with its sub attached where one exists. Rows with no sub stay listed on purpose — they are the work, not missing data. The question is not "where is the margin" but what is the least this obligation can be settled for, so a cover dearer than the sale is still a result. Actionable rows first, cheapest first; then the gaps, longest-unsettled first. Each listing is offered to one order only.

No sub names its reason, because three different situations look identical as a blank: unmapped (event unidentified — nothing was searched), pulling… (sources in flight), no match (searched; nothing had the same section, an equal-or-better row and a usable quantity — the only one meaning go find tickets), event not in catalogue (mapped to a real event we have never ingested, so nothing could be searched — the fix is to ingest the event, not to hunt inventory). A sub may be drawn from a larger listing when the seller's splits permit buying exactly what is owed; such a row says of N so you know the vendor console will show the whole lot. Where nothing sells the owed quantity at all, a lot one seat larger is bought whole and the row says buy N — the spare seat is paid for, and is already counted in the cost. Claim records an intent and hands you a fill sheet; it buys nothing — the purchase is made by hand in the vendor console. Live feed, refreshing every 60s. Sub filters the book: profitable only keeps just the covers that settle for less than the seat sold for, which necessarily hides every uncovered row.
